Our Organization & Heritage

Pioneering Pharmaceutical Integrity Across Africa

Built on a quarter-century of uncompromising pharmaceutical rigor, Vibtorah bridges world-class WHO-cGMP manufacturing with regional healthcare delivery to protect human lives.

Company Background & Origin

27 Years of Uncompromised Pharmaceutical Dedication

Vibtorah Pharmaceuticals was established with a singular mission: to ensure that hospitals, clinics, and families in West Africa have unwavering access to certified, authentic, high-potency medicines.

Starting from our central headquarters in Ilupeju, Lagos, the company recognized that combating counterfeit and degraded pharmaceutical products required a twofold commitment: forming direct manufacturing partnerships with WHO-cGMP certified plants, and constructing temperature-monitored distribution cold-chains across all geopolitical zones.

Today, our flagship formulations—including Artborah, Boraflox 500, Vitboram, and Vitboter—are stocked in major teaching hospitals, national referral clinics, and community pharmacies, while our New Age wellness line brings modern preventive nutrition to millions.

Guiding Principles

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our culture is governed by an unyielding ethical commitment to clinical safety, continuous scientific innovation, and community stewardship.

OUR MISSION

Protecting Lives Through Dependable Medicine

To develop, formulate, and distribute premium therapeutic medicines and wellness supplements with verified bioequivalence, making world-class healthcare universally accessible and affordable across African communities.

OUR VISION

The Continent's Benchmark of Quality

To stand as the most trustworthy and innovative pharmaceutical enterprise in Africa, recognized globally for zero-defect manufacturing, ethical distributor partnerships, and transformative community health initiatives.

Integrity

We hold ourselves to the highest ethical and regulatory standards, guaranteeing zero tolerance for compromised or unverified batch lots.

Quality

Adhering strictly to WHO-cGMP guidelines, pharmacopeial limits, and continuous stability audits under Zone IVb tropical conditions.

Accessibility

Ensuring life-saving therapies reach rural clinics and tier-2 towns with identical consistency as major teaching metropolitan centers.

Compassion
