Our Legacy of Trust

A Heritage of Quality, A Future of Innovation

Founded with a bold vision to eliminate substandard medicines and expand access to life-saving healthcare, Vitborah has grown into one of West Africa's most respected pharmaceutical leaders.

1998

Foundation & Core Mission

Established in Lagos with the primary mandate of importing and distributing verified, high-purity essential therapeutic pharmaceuticals.

2012

WHO-cGMP Manufacturing Alliances

Formed strategic technical partnerships with international WHO-cGMP manufacturing plants to formulate Artborah and Vitboter.

2018

Cold-Chain Logistics Expansion

Commissioned temperature-controlled regional distribution depots in Abuja, Port Harcourt, Onitsha, Kano, and Ibadan.

2024

Launch of New Age Wellness Line

Expanded into preventive healthcare and wellness with the launch of the New Age supplement portfolio.

2026 & Beyond

Continental Healthcare Reach

Expanding cross-border healthcare solutions, smart anti-counterfeiting tracking, and deepening grassroots charity outreach.
